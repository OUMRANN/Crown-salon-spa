Beauty that begins with a conversation.

Crown and Curl Salon & Spa is where Addis Ababa comes to be cared for, not just styled. Every visit begins with a consultation, because the most beautiful results start with understanding your hair, your skin, and the way you want to feel when you leave.

Our team brings professional expertise to every service, from precision styling and colour to restorative hair treatments, skin care, and spa rituals. We work in a calm, refined space where each detail, from the products we choose to the way we welcome you, is meant to feel effortless.

We also curate a collection of premium human hair, including raw hair and virgin bundles, chosen for quality, softness, and lasting wear. Whether you are preparing for an occasion or refreshing your everyday look, we shape a plan around you.
